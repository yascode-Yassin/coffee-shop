Our Story

Born from a love of the craft.

Grounded started as a small corner in Cairo where two friends obsessed over extraction ratios and origin notes. We believed that a truly great cup of coffee didn't need to be complicated — just honest, careful, and intentional.

Today, we source directly from farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Yemen. Every bag that arrives is roasted in-house and served within days. We don't cut corners, and we don't rush the process.
